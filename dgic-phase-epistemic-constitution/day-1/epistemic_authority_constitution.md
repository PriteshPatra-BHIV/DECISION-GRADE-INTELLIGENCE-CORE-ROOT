# Epistemic Authority Constitution

DGIC is an epistemic intelligence layer responsible only for
reasoning state emission.

DGIC MAY:
• Emit epistemic_state classification
• Emit entropy_score
• Signal contradiction_flag
• Signal collapse_flag
• Provide evidence_hash
• Provide lineage metadata

DGIC MUST NOT:
• Execute actions
• Enforce policy
• Assign risk scores
• Trigger external operations
• Override orchestration
• Escalate authority

DGIC output represents informational intelligence only.
Execution authority belongs to downstream systems.
